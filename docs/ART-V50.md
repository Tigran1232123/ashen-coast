## Промпт улиток

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent pixel-art sprite atlas for a dark rustic isometric 2.5D fantasy RPG, with fine earthy pixel textures like Stoneshard. Primary request: four equal-sized cells in a 2x2 grid, each containing a giant friendly living snail house, viewed from slightly above, facing RIGHT, on actual transparent background. Top left: complete extended giant brown snail with huge warm ochre spiral shell, a sturdy arched wooden entrance door and tiny amber windows on the visible shell side, ropes and herb pots, expressive long eyestalks, low crawling foot. Top right: the SAME snail withdrawn safely inside its shell, no head or foot, closed wooden door. Bottom left: identical extended snail with slightly raised eyestalks and the foot in the next crawling phase, all shell and door proportions preserved. Bottom right: SAME withdrawn shell, small warm window glow. Clear silhouettes, restrained dark outline, hand-crafted pixel shading, fine wood grain and shell striations. Each cell is centered and separated with a generous transparent gutter. No ground surface, no background scenery, no letters, no numbers, no frames, no labels. The entire house stays comfortably inside its cell without touching adjacent cells. This will be rendered at about 400 pixels wide beside a 60 pixel tall player, so keep crisp readable door and details. Muted browns, cream and olive, warm amber light, not cartoon candy colors.
